Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about what this version can do.

Which URLs are supported?

The page recognizes standard YouTube watch links, youtu.be short links, and YouTube Shorts links. A valid-looking URL doesn’t confirm that the video is accessible or downloadable.

Why might a URL fail?

It may be malformed, use an unsupported YouTube path, omit a valid video ID, or point to content that is unavailable. This page doesn’t check whether a video exists.

Which formats and qualities are available?

You can select MP4 or MP3 as a preference. Actual formats and quality choices depend on a connected processor; none are available in this standalone version.

Can I trim a video?

You can enter and validate a custom start and end time. The page can’t inspect the video duration or trim media until an authorized processor is connected.

Where is media processed?

Nowhere in this version. Your link is checked locally and no media is fetched. A future integration must clearly identify its processor and how it handles submitted links.

Why might downloading not be available?

Some content may be restricted by the platform, a creator, copyright, or local law. This site doesn’t bypass access controls. Download only when you have permission and the applicable terms allow it.
